# 5. Choose baselines

| Page | Role | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| https://zapier.com/blog/best-project-management-software-small-business/ | benchmark | Current US-facing roundup that shows the product categories and direct-answer style readers encounter. |
| https://www.sba.gov/blog/2018/2018-06/checklist-choosing-business-software/ | small-business lens | Government-hosted checklist focused on complexity, integrations, growth, and low-risk adoption. |
| https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/guideline-evaluate-project-management-software-4629 | methodology | Project-management-specific guidance for requirements, interface, integrations, scalability, and fit. |

**Why these three:** one product-oriented page and two criteria-oriented pages create a useful contrast. No second product roundup was needed; adding one would repeat the benchmark's role rather than fill a gap.
