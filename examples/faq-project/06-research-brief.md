```
Intent: informational with light commercial investigation
Target length: 1,150 words; enough for six answer-first questions plus a compact decision path
Baselines: Zapier — benchmark — current product roundup
           SBA — small-business lens — adoption and risk checklist
           PMI — methodology — requirements-first selection guidance

Must cover: define requirements; fit the workflow; usability and adoption; integrations; trial and growth; a simple decision path.
Differentiators: five-minute workflow map before demos (gap/format); exit test for export and cancellation (gap/angle); neutral answer to “is free enough?” (gap/angle).
Reader questions: what to look for (high); which views are needed (high); which integrations matter (high); whether free is enough (medium); how to test (high); how to plan for growth or switching (medium).
Terms: Kanban — cards moving through stages; timeline — work plotted against dates and dependencies; integration — a connection between tools; data portability — the ability to export and reuse records.
Pitfalls: choosing by feature count; treating a roundup's hands-on findings as universal; ignoring setup and adoption; forgetting support, export, and exit terms.
Structure advice: answer-first FAQ; six questions; no comparison table; FAQ yes.
Sensitive topic: no.
```
